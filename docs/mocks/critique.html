What works

AI

The foreground rocks hold real texture. The low, raking light gives the frame its only hard edges, and that contrast against the flat water is what makes the study read.

Keeping the horizon high leaves room for the rocks to lead the eye in from the bottom edge. The muted palette suits the quiet you asked for.

Technical

AI
Technical issue

The sky is close to clipping. The top third has almost no tonal separation. At ISO 100 and 1/250 s there was room to expose a stop under and lift the rocks in the edit instead.

Technical issueUncertain, judged from the preview

Sharpness seems to fall off at the nearest rocks. At f/8 on 24 mm, focusing about a third of the way into the scene should carry focus from the foreground through to the horizon. Check the full-resolution file.

Stylistic choice

The cool cast in the water reads as intentional and matches the rest of the palette. Leave it unless you want the rocks warmer; a global white-balance shift would pull both.

Composition and story

AI
Technical issue

The bright patch at the right edge pulls attention off the rocks. A slightly tighter crop, or half a stop off that edge in the edit, would settle it.

Stylistic choice

There is no resting point after the rocks, so the frame reads as a study of texture rather than of a place. That fits your brief. If you want it to feel like somewhere, one element in the middle distance would anchor it.

Top three improvements

AI · in priority order
  1. 1

    Protect the highlights

    Expose for the sky and bring the rocks up afterwards. The sensor has far more latitude in the shadows than in the highlights.

    Next shootMeter the sky, dial in −1 EV, and check the histogram for a gap at the right edge before you move on.

  2. 2

    Set focus for depth

    The nearest rocks are the subject, so they should be the sharpest thing in the frame.

    Next shootAt f/8, focus on a rock about a third of the way in, then check the far edge at 100% on the back of the camera.

  3. 3

    Quieten the right edge

    The bright patch competes with the rocks for attention and it is the easiest fix on this list.

    Next editTry a crop that ends at the last textured rock, or burn the right edge by half a stop.
